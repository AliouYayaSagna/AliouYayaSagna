Why go abroad?

During my Digital Sciences studies at ENSEEIHT, I am developing skills in artificial intelligence, Big Data, software, IoT and cybersecurity. An international experience would let me apply them in another environment, discover new working methods, and better understand the links between research, business and society.

Possible formats include an academic semester, an international internship, a double degree or a collaborative project. The report notes a minimum 17-week physical mobility period for students enrolled in a study program. The projects below depend on opportunities and mobility agreements available through the program.

01 · Montreal, Canada

University of Montreal / Mila

Project: Deepen my skills in artificial intelligence and machine learning in an international research environment.

What I want to learn

Machine learning, deep learning, data models and responsible AI. The University of Montreal and Mila ecosystem matches my interest in AI research and the path of Yoshua Bengio.

Possible activities

  • Take advanced courses in computer science and AI.
  • Join a research project or laboratory.
  • Experiment with a model on a concrete problem.
  • Present results as a report, demonstration or scientific poster.

Skills to develop: machine learning, scientific experimentation, data processing, English communication and international teamwork.

This would be my first immersion in a major global AI research ecosystem.

02 · Thies, Senegal

Thies Polytechnic School (EPT)

Project: Apply my computer science and data skills to a concrete problem in Senegal.

EPT offers engineering training in Computer Engineering and Telecommunications, as well as programs in software engineering and artificial intelligence. This mobility could connect my ENSEEIHT background with Senegal’s technological needs.

Project idea

Develop a digital prototype for one of these areas: education, health, transport, agriculture or access to information. The project could combine data collection, automated processing, a web interface and field deployment.

Skills to develop: solution design, software development, needs analysis, adaptation to local context and collaboration with students and field stakeholders.

The report connects this project to Ndiaye Dia and Wedji Kane: gaining technical expertise and turning it into a useful solution for the surrounding environment.

03 · Cranfield, United Kingdom

Cranfield University

Project: Connect artificial intelligence, connected systems and industry.

The report describes Cranfield’s applied AI, machine learning, data analysis, autonomous systems and digital transformation focus. It is a way to understand how technology moves from the laboratory to industrial applications.

Possible activities

  • Design an intelligent system for an industrial situation.
  • Work on data, IoT and automation.
  • Join a research or company project.
  • Study the technical, economic and human effects of a digital solution.

Skills to develop: intelligent systems, data engineering, IoT, project management, professional English and company relationships.

The report identifies Cranfield as a British partner in ENSEEIHT’s double-degree documents, making it a coherent option to explore.
